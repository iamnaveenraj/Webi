The Artist · अनामिका झा

A keeper of folk

Anamika Jha is one of the leading contemporary voices of Indian folk music, devoted to preserving the living traditions of the Gangetic plains.

"A song is a river — it belongs to everyone who lets it flow through them."

01

Born of Mithila

Raised amid the painted walls and paddy fields of Mithila, Anamika learned her first songs from the women of her village— lullabies, wedding sohars and songs of the harvest passed down through generations.

02

A voice, three tongues

She sings in Hindi, Maithili and Bhojpuri with equal intimacy, treating each language as a colour that expresses longing and joy in its own unique way.

03

From courtyard to stage

What began as courtyard gatherings became festival main stages. Today she performs across India, introducing regional folk traditions to audiences everywhere.

04

Keeping the archive alive

Beyond performing, she records and documents endangered folk traditions, ensuring Sohar, Kajri and Chaiti continue to inspire future generations.
