About

Preserving India's Folk Heritage

Anamika Jha is a renowned folk singer known for her soulful performances in Maithili, Bhojpuri and Hindi.

Through decades of performances, cultural festivals and live concerts, she continues to preserve the rich musical traditions of Mithila.
